KaziLaw

Tanzanian employment law, answered with citations. Free question-and-answer plus a compliance check that walks an employer through what the Acts require. Answers cite the section they come from, so the reader can verify them against the law itself.

KaziLaw is not a law firm and does not give legal advice. It is Tanzanian labour law, organized. kazilaw.com ↗

Chuma

A training app written for East Africa rather than translated into it: Swahili first, bodyweight-first programming for people without a gym, and East African food in the nutrition side. Rotating trainer-led programs, set-by-set logging, and a licensed exercise library served from our own infrastructure instead of a third-party quota.

Sema & Sema-API

Customer-facing AI for small businesses, with the guardrails written down instead of hoped for. Each industry pack declares what the assistant must never invent and when it has to hand off to a person — the pharmacy pack refuses dosage, drug interactions, and diagnosis outright. Blank onboarding fields are fed back into the prompt, so the model is told what it does not know.

The training-program generator extracted from Chuma and released on its own. Infrastructure

We run our own hardware, and our own agents on it.

Two systems we built for ourselves and run in production. Neither is public — this page is the only place they're described.

Internal

OpenClaw

The agent runtime our automation runs on. Long-lived agents with their own memory, scheduled work, and tool access, sitting directly on our servers rather than behind someone else's platform — so the jobs keep their state and we keep the logs.

Internal

Hermes

The agent that directs production work: media pipelines, batch generation, and the routine operational jobs that would otherwise be a person copying numbers between systems. It briefs the tools, checks the output, and reports what it did.

Servers
Dedicated and virtual hosts we administer ourselves, running 15+ containerized services.
Data
Postgres with pgvector for retrieval, on databases we can back up and inspect.
Inference
Self-hosted image, video, and speech models on our own GPU, alongside hosted APIs where they earn their cost.
Operations
Daily reporting automation for a three-location restaurant group: sales digests, cost variance flagging, and tax-receipt tracking.
